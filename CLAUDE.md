# claudepdotj

Repo for Prathamesh's (pdotj.co / @p_dot_j) content and automation work.

## Writing rule

Any human-facing document or copy produced in this repo (Reels scripts,
carousels, captions, emails, WhatsApp broadcasts, landing page copy, docs)
must run through the `humanize-writing` skill as a final pass before it's
considered done — after brand-voice drafting, before shipping. This does not
apply to code, comments, or internal notes.

## Copywriting rule: mirror the internal monologue

For any persuasive or sales copy (ads, WhatsApp broadcasts, emails,
carousels, Reel hooks, landing pages) — open by mirroring the reader's exact
internal monologue in the present moment: what they're literally thinking or
feeling right now (scrolling, comparing themselves to others, doubting
themselves, tired of hype), stated as a direct claim, not a vague tease.
Land that first, then make the point.

This is what makes the reader feel seen before the belief-shift, and it's
what makes the belief-shift land harder. It's a standing default for
anything whose job is to persuade — not a one-off technique to apply only
when asked. Skip it for value-drop or informational content where there's no
belief to shift.
