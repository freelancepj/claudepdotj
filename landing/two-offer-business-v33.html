Each module takes about 20–30 minutes. Do one a day, and your plan is done in about a week.

The model doesn't depend on your field. My clients use it in marketing, language coaching, food, medicine, accounting and more.

No. The guide gives you the tools, prompts and examples to do it yourself. Personal feedback is part of my 1:1 mentorship.
