1. Connect Clarity (steps are in the chat). Then we can see why 95 of 100
   visitors leave the sales page without opening the pop-up. Maybe they stop
   scrolling, get stuck on something, or the page is hard to use on mobile.
2. Decide what the pop-up is for. If it's there to sort people for your
   ₹30K+ offer, let everyone buy the ₹499 product and save their answer as a
   GHL tag for follow-up. Today it blocks honest buyers while everyone else
   gets past it anyway.
3. Check 2OB. If 2OB sales happen outside GHL, tell me where so I can count
   them. If they don't, the 2OB campaigns cost ₹2,818 this week with nothing
   to show for it.

All numbers are small (a few sales a day), so one sale moves the rates a lot.
Treat these patterns as early clues. They'll get clearer as the twice-a-day
reports pile up.
